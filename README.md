📖 Project Overview
Our travel diary explores destinations across Japan, the Philippines, Georgia, and Greenland. It highlights each country's famous landmarks, natural beauty, and cultural experiences through an engaging visual journey.
🌍 Countries & Destinations Featured
Our journey spans four countries across islands, mountains, neon cities, and arctic ice:
1. Japan (Tokyo to Tomorrow): Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto), Mount Fuji, and Suga Shrine Stairs (Tokyo).
2. Philippines (Sea to Snow): Chocolate Hills (Bohol) and El Nido (Palawan).
3. Georgia (Sea to Snow): Tbilisi Old Town & Narikala Fortress, and Gergeti Trinity Church (Kazbegi).
4. Greenland (Sea to Snow): Ilulissat Icefjord and the Northern Lights (Aurora).

🎨 Design System (Colors & Fonts)
The website uses a dark navy background with warm cream text, complemented by country-inspired accent colours.
- Main Colours: Dark Navy and Warm Cream
- Country Accent Colours: Vermilion Red (Japan), Tropical Teal (Philippines), and Glacier Blue (Greenland)
- Typography: Georgia, with Times New Roman as a fallback font.
